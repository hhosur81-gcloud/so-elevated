# Enterprise Employee Leave and Absence Policy 2026

## Section 3.1: Paid Time Off (PTO) Accrual
Full-time salaried employees accrue 160 hours (20 business days) of PTO per calendar year, accrued at 13.33 hours per calendar month. Maximum rollover into the following calendar year is capped at 80 hours.

## Section 3.2: Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to up to 5 consecutive paid business days for the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling), and up to 3 paid business days for extended family members. Additional unpaid time may be coordinated with your HR Business Partner.

## Section 3.3: Parental Leave
Eligible employees receive up to 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Parental leave must be taken within the first 12 months.

## Section 3.4: Short-Term Medical Leave of Absence (LOA)
For non-work-related illnesses or medical procedures exceeding 3 consecutive business days, employees may initiate a Short-Term Medical Leave of Absence for up to 12 weeks with 100% salary continuation under company disability coverage upon medical certification.
